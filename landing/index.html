How it works

From a phone photo to an evidence-backed verdict

01

Capture and straighten

Canny edges, contour and a four-corner fit locate the note. A perspective warp turns any angle into a clean, fixed-size view.

02

Inspect every feature

Nine focused checks each produce a score and a picture: the thread, the print, the texture, the serial numbers and the watermark.

03

Weakest-link verdict

Scores are blended with extra weight on the two weakest checks, so one clearly failed feature cannot be hidden by strong ones.

The checks

Nine features, nine pieces of evidence

1

Security thread

Finds the unbroken vertical thread and measures how continuous it is.

missing or broken thread
2

Print sharpness

Fine-line detail measured at a fixed scale, so photo resolution does not skew it.

soft photocopies
3

Halftone and moiré

An FFT spectrum exposes the regular dot grid left by inkjet and laser printers.

home reprints
4

Ink saturation

Genuine inks are vivid and specific. Faded or black-and-white copies are not.

desaturated copies
5

Intaglio texture

Local Binary Patterns compare the portrait’s raised-ink micro-texture with a genuine note.

flat reproductions
6

Reference match

ORB features and RANSAC confirm the design geometry matches a genuine reference.

wrong design
7

Serial numbers

The number is printed twice. OCR reads both panels, tolerant of look-alike letters, and they must agree.

invalid or mismatched
8

Watermark window

In normal light the window must be blank. A visible portrait there was printed on the surface.

printed watermark
9

Watermark, backlit

Against light, a soft portrait must sit inside the paper itself.

no real watermark
Hold it to the light

The watermark check, done the way people do it

Most people tilt a note toward a lamp to find the Gandhi watermark. The Inspector does the same with a second, optional photo taken with light passing through the note.

Two checks work together. The backlit photo shows that a smooth portrait lives inside the paper. The normal-light photo shows the window is blank, so nothing was just printed on top.

Get started

Run it in two minutes

pip install -r requirements.txt
brew install tesseract      # optional, enables the serial-number check
streamlit run app.py

Put one or two clean genuine note photos in references/, upload a photo, and use the attack simulator to watch the verdict react to a photocopy or inkjet reprint. Calibrate thresholds on your own data with evaluate.py.

An honest note. This is a screening aid, not a certification tool. Phone cameras cannot capture UV, microprinting or tactile features, the defaults target the ₹500 Mahatma Gandhi series, and the watermark thresholds need calibration on real backlit photos. Suspicious notes should be verified by a bank.
